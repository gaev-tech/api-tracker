Direction · Revised

Less futuristic HUD, more printed emblem. The language now borrows from constructivist posters and heraldry: flat pictograms locked in circles, heavy condensed type, stencil numerals, diagonal ribbon banners.

Color is loud and high-contrast — ultramarine blue leads every action, vermilion red is the brand's signature accent (never an “error” when it sits in buttons or inputs), gold marks highlights, and cream gives us a paper surface for inverted, poster-style moments.
